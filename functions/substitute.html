          term: LambdaTerm,

          alphaConversionStrategy: AlphaConversionStrategy,
      ): LambdaTerm

      Substitutes "replacement" for every free occurrence of "variable" in "term", alpha-converting bound variables as needed to avoid capturing free variables in "replacement".

      Parameters

      • term: LambdaTerm

        The term to substitute into.

      • variable: LambdaVariable

        The variable being replaced.

      • replacement: LambdaTerm

        The term to substitute in place of "variable".

      • existingVariableNames: ReadonlySet<string>

        The display names of every variable appearing anywhere in the whole term being reduced this step, used to pick a collision-free name when alpha-converting.

      • alphaConversionStrategy: AlphaConversionStrategy

        Decides which fresh variable to use when alpha-conversion is needed.

      Returns LambdaTerm

      "term" with the substitution applied.

      Remarks

      Exported because both LambdaCalculus and DeterministicLambdaCalculus need it: it is the one piece of reduction logic (capture-avoiding substitution) whose correctness is subtle and whose behavior must be identical regardless of which system's evaluation strategy (full vs. weak) is driving the reduction. The two systems are otherwise independent ComputationSystem implementations, not related by inheritance, since their "start" and "isStopped" contracts differ.
